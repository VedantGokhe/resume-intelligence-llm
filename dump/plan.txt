==============================================================

JSON matching a strict Pydantic contract.

  Method  : QLoRA fine-tuning (4-bit quantized base + LoRA adapters)
  Hardware: Google Colab Free Tier — NVIDIA T4 (16 GB VRAM)
  Download: Hugging Face Hub (Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B)
